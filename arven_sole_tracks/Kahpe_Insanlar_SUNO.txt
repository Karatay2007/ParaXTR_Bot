# Arven Solé — Kahpe İnsanlar

# Suno: Nefesim Daralıyor winning recipe

============================================================
STYLES (kopyala)
============================================================
Hard-hitting Turkish emotional hit, elektro bağlama lead upfront, aggressive driving drums, punchy bass, exciting midtempo groove, rhythm hits hard, not slow, not flat, addictive catchy chorus, male vocal, verse quieter then chorus explodes, rich arrangement, clean studio, arabesk emotion with rock energy

============================================================
EXCLUDE
============================================================
slow ballad, soft sleepy, flat dynamics, weak drums, trap 808 spam, EDM, cute, happy

============================================================
SETTINGS
============================================================
Vocal: Male
Weirdness: ~45%
Style Influence: ~90%
Audio reference: YOK
